Biko: A Sweet Taste of Filipino Tradition

Biko is a traditional Filipino rice cake loved for its sticky texture, sweet flavor, and rich coconut aroma. Made with glutinous rice, coconut milk, and brown sugar, this delicious delicacy is a favorite at family gatherings and special celebrations.

The secret to delicious biko is the way the ingredients blend together. The glutinous rice becomes soft and chewy, while the coconut milk and brown sugar create a rich, caramel-like sweetness. Every bite is soft, sticky, and filled with comforting flavor.

One of the most recognizable features of biko is its golden-brown topping, often made from latik or a thick caramelized coconut mixture. This topping adds extra sweetness and a deeper coconut flavor that makes the rice cake even more irresistible.

In the Philippines, biko is often prepared during birthdays, fiestas, holidays, and family reunions. It is commonly placed on a large tray and sliced into squares, making it easy to share with relatives, friends, and visitors.

More than just a sweet rice cake, biko represents the warmth of Filipino hospitality and the joy of sharing homemade food. Its familiar sweetness brings back memories of celebrations, family kitchens, and moments spent together around the table.
