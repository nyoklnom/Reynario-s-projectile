Adobong Pusit: A Taste of the Sea

Adobong pusit is a popular Filipino seafood dish known for its savory sauce, tender squid, and distinctive dark color. Cooked with vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, onions, and pepper, this delicious dish brings together the bold flavors of traditional Filipino adobo and the natural freshness of the sea.

Preparing adobong pusit begins with cleaning the squid carefully while preserving its ink sac for the sauce. Garlic and onions are sautéed until fragrant, creating a flavorful foundation for the dish. The squid is then added along with the seasonings, allowing the ingredients to blend together beautifully.

Vinegar gives the dish its signature tangy taste, while soy sauce adds a rich and savory flavor. The squid ink creates the sauce's characteristic dark appearance and adds a distinctive seafood taste. Careful cooking helps keep the squid tender and prevents it from becoming overly chewy.

Adobong pusit is best enjoyed hot with freshly cooked white rice. Its flavorful sauce seeps into every grain, making each bite satisfying and delicious. Some cooks add tomatoes or chili peppers for extra flavor, giving the dish a personal touch while keeping its traditional Filipino character.

More than just a seafood meal, adobong pusit represents the creativity of Filipino home cooking. It transforms simple ingredients into a dish full of character, flavor, and tradition. Whether prepared for a family lunch or a special dinner, this savory squid dish brings the comforting taste of Filipino cuisine straight to the table.
